# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, acknowledge
sources, disclose uncertainty and prioritise safety when discussing industrial systems.
Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation and
publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the security contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer may edit, reject or remove contributions and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.

This compact policy is adapted from the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
